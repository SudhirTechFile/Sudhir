## Introduction
This dashboard was created for Job seeker, Job Transistor and swapper to solve a common problem information about the data job market using chop fluctuate data from 2024 job posting fact and provide easy single usage interactive market trend dashboard with compensation. 

## Key Features 
Global KPIs: At-a-glance visualization of total tracked vacancies (479K Job Count), overall median yearly salary ($113K), and median hourly rate ($47.62).

Market Trend Analysis: A interactive line chart mapping Jobs Over Time to highlight seasonal hiring patterns and market contractions/expansions.

Competitive Landscape Matrix: An Hourly vs. Median Salary scatter plot that maps role seniority against compensation, clearly identifying high-value specializations (e.g., Machine Learning Engineer vs. Data Analyst).

Comprehensive Job Stats: A deeply detailed data matrix showing exact job counts, median salaries, and micro sparklines tracking individual role trends over time.

Job Title Drill-Through (Granular View)
Allows deep-dive exploration into specific roles (e.g., Business Analyst).

Advanced Salary Benchmarking: Utilizes gauge charts to display compensation ranges, instantly communicating where the median falls within the broader market minimums and maximums.

Benefit & Requirement Profiling: Tracks workplace flexibility and prerequisites using donut charts to isolate metrics like Work From Home %, No Degree Mention %, and Health Insurance %.

Sourcing & Schedule Dynamics: Uses bar charts to identify dominant job platforms (e.g., LinkedIn vs. BeBee) and treemaps to analyze employment classification structures (Full-Time vs. Contractor).

📈 Technical Achievements & Solutions
Drill-Through Architecture: Built a seamless UX flow allowing users to right-click a job role on the executive summary and instantly land on a specialized, filtered page detailing that exact role's specific market characteristics.

Data Integrity & Maintenance: Showcased a proactive approach to dashboard optimization, identifying cloud visual boundary limitations (e.g., Azure Maps tenant configurations) to plan enterprise-grade compliance updates.

## Conclusion 
This is the dashboard showcase how Power BI can transform the raw data of job posting data flagged into a powerful tool for career analysis.
